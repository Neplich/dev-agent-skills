# Jira research record

- Captured: 2026-08-06
- Sources: `https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira`, public Jira pricing and
  product pages, Atlassian marketplace pages, and approved sales-call notes.

## Product facts

Jira is positioned as a flexible work-management system with deep support for
software planning, issue tracking, reporting, automation, and configurable
workflows. Public packaging includes Free, Standard, Premium, and Enterprise
tiers. Its audience ranges from individual software teams to large enterprises
using several Atlassian products.

Current public messaging emphasizes bringing business and software teams into
shared planning, using automation and AI capabilities, and extending the system
through the Atlassian platform and marketplace.

## Customer and sales themes

- Positive themes: mature workflow configuration, reporting, permissions,
  enterprise administration, and a large integration marketplace.
- Concern themes: configuration and administration cost, inconsistent setups
  across teams, and a heavier interaction model for teams that need only a
  focused product-development workflow.
- Jira's common pitch is that one configurable platform can support complex
  work across teams while preserving governance and reporting.
- Three recurring differentiators in calls are workflow flexibility,
  enterprise governance, and ecosystem breadth.

## Competitive conversation notes

Prospects favor Jira when existing Atlassian adoption, regulated controls, or
specialized workflows outweigh simplicity. Losses occur when teams prioritize
fast onboarding and a consistent product-development experience over breadth.
Ask which custom fields and automations affect real decisions, who maintains
them, and what the annual administration cost is. Do not claim that every Jira
deployment is slow or overconfigured; validate the customer's actual instance
during a proof of concept.
