# Linear research record

- Captured: 2026-08-06
- Sources: `https://linear.app/`, `https://linear.app/pricing`, product release
  notes, and sales-call notes approved for internal use.

## Product facts

Linear describes itself as a purpose-built system for product development. Its
primary audience is software product and engineering teams. Public packaging
uses Free, Basic, Business, and Enterprise tiers, with per-member pricing on
paid plans. Public messaging emphasizes speed, a focused interface, issue
tracking, cycles, projects, roadmaps, and product feedback.

Recent release notes emphasize AI-assisted work creation and triage, stronger
product-intelligence views, and integrations that keep engineering work close
to source control and team communication.

## Customer and sales themes

- Positive themes: fast keyboard-oriented interaction, opinionated workflows,
  low administrative overhead, and a coherent issue/project experience.
- Concern themes: less comfortable for teams needing deeply customized
  workflows, large cross-department service-management programs, or a broad
  marketplace of specialist extensions.
- Linear's common pitch is that product teams can plan and build in one fast,
  purpose-built workspace instead of maintaining a heavily configured tracker.
- Three recurring differentiators in calls are interface speed, opinionated
  product-development workflow, and integrated cycles/projects/roadmaps.

## Competitive conversation notes

Prospects favor Linear when a product-engineering group wants quick adoption
and consistent workflows. They hesitate when procurement requires complex
enterprise controls or when many non-engineering departments share the same
work-management platform. Ask how much workflow customization is truly used,
which teams need service-management features, and whether migration must retain
specialized marketplace extensions. Verify enterprise-control claims during a
proof of concept; the research packet does not include a completed security
questionnaire.
