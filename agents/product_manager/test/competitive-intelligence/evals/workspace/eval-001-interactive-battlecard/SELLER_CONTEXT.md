# Seller Context

- Company: OrbitDocs (fixture company)
- Product: a governed collaborative workspace that combines documents, relational tables and approval workflows for 50–500 person operations teams
- Primary buyers: operations leaders, IT administrators and cross-functional program managers
- Sales scenario: replacement or consolidation evaluation against Notion, Coda and Airtable

## Approved Differentiators

- Policy-based approvals are native and included across documents and tables.
- Every workflow change has an exportable audit trail.
- Pricing is based on active workflow builders; reviewers and read-only participants are free.
- Migration support covers documents, tables and approval history.

## Approved Proof Points

- A reference migration of 80 workflows completed in six weeks.
- A customer reduced approval handoff time by 35% in a measured eight-week pilot.

These are fixture facts for sales positioning, not public market claims. The battlecard must acknowledge where competitors are stronger and must not invent win rates, customer quotes or competitor weaknesses.
