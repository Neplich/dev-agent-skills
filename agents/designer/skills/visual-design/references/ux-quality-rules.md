# UX Quality Checks

Review the actual artifact against the needs of its users and devices.

- Readable contrast, visible focus, meaningful control labels, and keyboard
  operation support accessible use.
- Touch targets have comfortable hit areas; pressed, loading, and disabled
  states give clear feedback.
- Responsive layouts preserve content priority and usable navigation across
  target viewports.
- Charts and status indicators combine color with readable text or symbols.
- Forms place useful errors near the relevant field and explain recovery.
- Empty states identify a useful next action; longer waits communicate progress.
- Motion communicates change and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Spacing, icon treatment, elevation, and component states form a consistent
  system while supporting the needs of dense and spacious surfaces.
